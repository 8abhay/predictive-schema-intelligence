## Important finding: ACM is no longer free-by-default
From 1 Jan 2026 all ACM journals and proceedings are open access. Authors pay an article processing charge unless the **corresponding author's institution participates in ACM Open** or a waiver applies (ACM newsletter, conference notices). ACM stated that India's national "One Nation One Subscription" agreement covers about 6,500 publicly funded institutions, and ACM describes geographic and hardship waiver policies. **As an independent researcher you have no ACM Open institution, so assume an ACM APC applies unless a waiver is granted.** One ACM-affiliated conference's 2024 policy page said India-based authors with no institutional affiliation could apply for a financial waiver after verification; that was specific to that conference, and ACM's general discretionary waivers are described as the exception, not the rule. An ACM venue may therefore cost money (conference notices for 2026 cite $250-$350 for 2026 conference proceedings; journal APCs were reported much higher). I did not verify India-specific waiver eligibility.
